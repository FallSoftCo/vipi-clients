# Verified purchase restore after Vipi-only deletion/rejoin

Migration `20261117_05` follows global deletion04. The existing profile UUID remains disposable: rejoin creates a new account and does not resurrect device credentials, vault identity, deleted profile data or trial time. New purchase attribution and entitlement response fields retain their current UUID-based contract.

`billing_account_bindings` retains only `(store, binding_hash, owner_hash)` while the underlying Fallsoft identity exists. `owner_hash` uses the authority-namespaced trial-eligibility HMAC; `binding_hash` is a separate app/store-domain HMAC of the Apple canonical UUID or exact Google obfuscated account identifier. There are no raw identities, profile UUIDs, device IDs, transaction IDs, purchase tokens, products, expiry dates or access grants in this table. App-only deletion captures known billing identifiers even without an Entitlement because a payment or approval may be ahead of its initial synchronization. It then erases Entitlement and encrypted provider references as before. Hashes persist beyond the bounded deletion receipt so legitimate restore does not expire after seven days. Do not truncate a customer's historical binding list; verification performs one exact indexed lookup, not list materialization.

StoreKit signature validation and current subscription-status verification remain required. Google still uses the authoritative subscriptionsv2 response; product, annual plan, environment, current state, linked purchase chain, acknowledgement and sandbox-user restrictions remain in their existing provider path. Historical billing ownership does not grant access or sandbox privileges. Unknown/missing account fields fail, and neither email nor possession of a store account transfers a purchase to another Fallsoft identity.

Every explicit sync captures the authenticated principal's exact current subject and UUID before network verification. Publication takes the same eligibility-HMAC lifecycle lock as deletion/linking, before the User row lock, and rechecks that subject/UUID and global hold. Deletion/rejoin or identity linking crossing the provider read causes a retry without grant or acknowledgement. Worker refresh captures/rechecks its current owner too. Lost lease or older observation is now an explicit no-op result; it cannot newly acknowledge a purchase. Explicit sync reports a conflict on that no-op; workers retain their existing retry/lease handling.

Explicit dual-proof legacy→Fallsoft linking seeds/rekeys only ownership hashes inside the existing target-before-source locks. It preserves the profile UUID and paid grants in place. Global deletion deletes canonical and retained alias ownership hashes in the same transaction, including hashes newly seeded by `request_deletion`; then it removes trial eligibility/aliases. Global holds remain and block old-token/profile resurrection. No store or WorkOS identity mutation is part of this restore fix.

The iOS client now submits a locally verified transaction with a recognized product to its authenticated backend even when the transaction keeps an old profile's app account token. Backend ownership decides whether this exact identity may restore. The current-account snapshot UUID guard and Transaction.finish-after-success ordering remain. A definite server ownership rejection sets accountConflicts, clears the pending-confirmation state and prevents another charge; a rightful successful retry clears that conflict. Uncertain/network failures stay unfinished. Android already submits purchases for backend ownership verification, so no charge-flow change is needed.

Privacy disclosures now name this persistent pseudonymous ownership linkage and its erasure through global deletion. This is not an anonymous record or an entitlement retained after deletion. Migration downgrade refuses to lose populated binding evidence. The recovery importer does not yet apply shared links/global holds/billing erasures completely: `required_before_reopen` explicitly includes ownership-binding and post-snapshot global-erasure reconciliation. Do not reopen a restored service or claim complete shared-account recovery from this change.

Deploy05 before enabling app-only deletion/shared migration. There is no historical deletion-receipt importer or expired-receipt fallback. If an identity/profile/billing mapping was already irreversibly erased before this policy, a store proof alone does not reconstruct its former Fallsoft ownership. Assess the actual production schema/data before rollout rather than weakening ownership.
