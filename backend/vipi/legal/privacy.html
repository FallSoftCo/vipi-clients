What happens to a message

Your phone encrypts a direct message with the Signal Protocol before sending it. Vipi’s relay delivers encrypted data. Your friend’s phone decrypts it locally. The relay has no key to read your conversation. Each participant can keep or share their own copy.

To deliver messages, Vipi handles account, device and message identifiers, public encryption keys, recipients, times and delivery status. A typing indicator shares a temporary start or stop signal. A read receipt tells the sender a message appeared on your screen. Your draft stays on your phone.

A group draft stays on your phone until you choose to create the group. Where group messaging is available, your phone encrypts each group message for the other members’ devices. Vipi can see the group name, who belongs to it, their roles, invitations and delivery records so it can manage membership and deliver messages.

Where media sharing is available in one-to-one chats, you can choose a photo or video, use the camera, or record a voice note with the microphone. Your phone encrypts the file and sends the key to your friend inside the encrypted message. The relay handles the encrypted file, its size, a fingerprint of the encrypted bytes and the devices allowed to retrieve it. If you choose a profile photo, Vipi can send a small copy or a removal update inside a later encrypted direct message.

Sign-in, usernames and invitations

WorkOS handles sign-in for Fallible Software, including your email address and authentication records. Vipi uses an account identifier, profile creation date, chosen username and display name, and device registration to run your account. Someone with your exact username can find your Vipi profile. A personal invitation link includes that username. Username lookup does not expose your sign-in email.

You can enter a username or share your own link without giving Vipi access to your contacts. If you allow contact access, Vipi reads names, phone numbers and email addresses on your device so you can pick a recipient. Your address book stays there. Vipi does not upload it, match it against a server directory, or include it in history exports.

Selecting a phone number or email address opens an addressed draft in your messaging or mail app. You choose whether to send it. Your carrier or mail provider handles the invitation you send. You can revoke contact access in your phone’s settings.

Language practice on your device

After a message opens, Vipi prepares translations and reading guides on your phone. Shallow introduces practice words. Deep shows whole messages. Your phone keeps the original wording and can save translations, reading guides and learned words in encrypted local storage. Speech uses local voices or speech engines.

Downloading a language model or voice requires a connection. Apple and Google may receive download or operational data. Language and notification software may send installation or device identifiers, app and operating-system versions, language or model selections, detected language tags, performance and errors. Google’s on-device translation does not send the text being translated or its translation to Google.

Read Google’s Android ML Kit disclosures, its Apple-platform disclosures and Firebase’s privacy information.

Notifications and previews

Vipi stores an encrypted push token to reach your device through Apple Push Notification service or Google’s Firebase Cloud Messaging. A push carries a generic alert or inbox-change signal. On supported iPhones, it can also carry a short-lived encrypted reference. The registered device can use that reference with its own credentials to fetch the encrypted direct message and prepare the preview locally. Your friend’s name and message text are not in the push request. Android can register for background delivery even with visible alerts off. On iPhone, registration follows your notification permission and Vipi settings.

If your version and settings support message previews, your device can show the sender and decrypted text, including on a lock screen. A generic alert may appear if previews are hidden or the message is unavailable. Existing installations keep previous privacy choices. On iPhone, banners inside Vipi have a separate setting. Check Vipi’s notification settings and your phone’s lock-screen settings.

Optional feedback and earlier purchases

If Vipi shows you the optional funding question, you can answer Yes, Not sure or No, or choose Skip. Vipi saves your choice with your account and the question identifier to avoid asking again. The app uses local activity to decide when to show it. Your message text, activity counts and gesture history are not submitted with the choice. Deleting your Vipi account removes the response. Answering never purchases anything. Apple and Google handle store ratings separately.

The launch version offers free friend chat, including groups where available. If you previously bought a subscription, the store handles payment. Vipi may receive a product identifier, a purchase or transaction reference, expiration and revocation status to reconcile that purchase. Vipi does not receive your payment card details.

What you share when you report someone

You can block a person to stop new direct messages between the two accounts. Where groups are available, blocking a person also leaves your shared groups. If you report a direct message, you can review the original text you choose to send. Vipi receives the selected content, account and message identifiers, reason and timing for review. Other messages in the conversation stay private.

Reported text is encrypted in service storage. Authorized reviewers can read the submitted evidence and may restrict an account. A report alone does not prove authorship or automatically suspend someone. For safety concerns or a review of a restriction, write to one@fallsoft.co.

How long records are kept

Message delivery
Undelivered direct-message ciphertext has a 72-hour window from message creation. Delivery acknowledgement, cancellation or expiry clears the ciphertext. Routing, delivery and read records are removed seven days after the delivery window ends. The expiry job normally runs about once a minute. An outage can delay cleanup.
Photos, videos and voice notes
Encrypted uploads have a 72-hour window. Sending a message gives the recipient’s device up to 72 hours from message creation to fetch the attachment. Expired files are queued for deletion. A file already saved on a participant’s device can remain there.
Safety reports
Submitted reports expire after 30 days. Account deletion also removes reports associated with that account. Separate restriction and review records help us enforce account safety.
Store events
Notification IDs and fingerprints used to prevent duplicate processing expire after 30 days. These duplicate-check records contain neither the notification body nor a purchase token.
Deletion receipts
A minimal receipt is kept for seven days so you can check that deletion finished.

Encrypted service backups can contain account and sign-in linkage records, device registrations, prior purchase, usage and safety records, and any saved funding-survey choice. They exclude relayed messages, push tokens, public messaging-key records and submitted report text. The backup policy for groups and media also excludes group names, membership, invitations and group delivery records, along with attachment files, transfer records and download permissions. The backup policy retains up to three verified generations as new backups succeed. A generation has no fixed duration. Restoring service must respect completed deletions.

The public Vipi website logs IP addresses, requested URLs, browser details and referrers for security. Website logs rotate daily with up to 14 archived rotations. Read the website privacy notice for that site.

Your backups and deletion choices

You can export direct-message text, dates and saved Vipi friend details to a password-encrypted file. Choose where to save it. If you choose a storage provider, it receives that encrypted file. Vipi cannot recover its password. The export leaves out group conversations, attachment files, profile photos, drafts waiting to send, sign-in credentials, live encryption keys, learned words and your phone’s address book. Restore it under the same Vipi account.

Use Settings in the app or delete your Vipi account online. Deletion removes your Vipi profile, registered device and keys, pending messages, account usage and access records, and associated reports and survey responses where present. A web request cannot erase history on a phone. Manage local app data, backups you exported, and any store renewal separately. Your friend keeps their own copies.

For earlier linked sign-ins, trial eligibility or purchases, keyed identifiers and, where applicable, trial dates can remain while the shared FallSoft account exists to prevent duplicate identities or incorrect historical claims. These records cannot be used to read messages. Your shared FallSoft sign-in and other FallSoft apps are separate. Ask privacy@fallsoft.co about deletion across services.

Who handles your data and how to reach us

Fallible Software, Co. operates Vipi. We use Hetzner for service hosting, WorkOS for sign-in, and Apple and Google for notifications and applicable language or store services. Processing may take place outside your country. We use information to provide and secure Vipi, respond to requests and meet legal obligations. If you contact support, we receive the details you choose to send. We may share information when legally required, as described in the company policy. We do not sell personal information or use advertising trackers.

For access, correction, deletion or a privacy concern, email privacy@fallsoft.co. We may need to verify the request. Applicable law and necessary recordkeeping affect which requests we can fulfill. Vipi is not directed to children under 13. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided information.
