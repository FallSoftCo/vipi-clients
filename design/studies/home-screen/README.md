# Vipi identity on a home screen

The signature and songbird are two original proposals for the app icon. The signature uses four individually drawn letters, a long descending p and a gold dot over the final i. The songbird uses a resting profile and an open beak, with a gold throat that echoes a learned word in a message.

Switch between both proposals and the earlier speech mark in the same launcher cell. The interactive study preserves the neighboring icons, wallpaper and labels from native QA simulator captures. [Compare on iPhone and Pixel](https://system76-pc.tailb77f2a.ts.net/8bar/vipi-studio-20261120/launcher.html)

Both SVGs here are editable. The shipping launcher assets have their own platform exports.

The selected direction uses the Vipi lettering. In the question-wrap treatment a single open curve embraces all four letters and ends in a separate dot. “Vipi?” is recorded as an informal Swahili greeting among peers. [Recorded greeting](https://www.langmedia.fivecolleges.edu/resources/tanzania/basic-communications/informal-greetings-among-youths)
