Vipi bundles 57 local speech profiles for its eSpeak NG fallback.

Mandarin pronunciation data in Vipi’s compiled eSpeak NG dictionary incorporates Unihan kMandarin readings from the Unicode Consortium and compound readings from CC-CEDICT contributors, with corrections incorporated by eSpeak NG, including contributions from the Ekho project.
