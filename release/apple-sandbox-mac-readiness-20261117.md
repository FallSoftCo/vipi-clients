# Apple sandbox purchase qualification — read-only preparation

Apple documents TestFlight testing of iPhone and iPad apps on Apple silicon Macs. It is enabled per tester group; app-level Mac availability and minimum macOS compatibility also apply. The existing company Mac reports Apple M1 Pro, arm64, macOS 26.6 and a TestFlight application. Hardware and application presence do not establish that this build is available to a tester, compatible, or can complete StoreKit and Vipi workflows on that Mac. The retained build 1.0 (2) receipt recorded completed upload/processing but Missing Compliance and zero tester groups at its readback; current compliance/group state needs the Apple owner's fresh readback.

[Apple: Test iPhone and iPad apps on Apple silicon Macs](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/test-a-beta-version/test-iphone-and-ipad-apps-on-macs-with-apple-silicon/) · [Mac availability](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-your-apps-availability/manage-availability-of-iphone-and-ipad-apps-on-macs-with-apple-silicon)

Apple states that TestFlight purchases use its Sandbox, do not charge, and can use real configured App Store products and server-signed transactions. TestFlight membership is distinct from the Vipi sign-in and its backend user UUID; a store tester's identity alone does not grant Vipi admission or sandbox receipt eligibility. Apple lists an active Paid Applications Agreement and configured products among sandbox prerequisites. Verify actual agreement/product/group/region state rather than inferring it from the signed IPA. Accessing optional sandbox controls may require changing Media & Purchases sign-in; Apple notes this can affect access to purchased content, so avoid changing the company Mac account without a deliberate coordinated decision.

[Apple: sandbox purchases](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/storekit/testing-in-app-purchases-with-sandbox) · [App Review sandbox preparation](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-review/before-submitting-for-review/configure-in-app-purchases)

Apple also documents real App Store Sandbox testing on Simulator for development, whereas local StoreKit Testing in Xcode uses Xcode-signed transactions and does not prove the backend's App Store signature-verification path. The uploaded signed iOS IPA is not itself a Simulator build, and no Simulator or Mac purchase has been demonstrated here. [Apple: Xcode, Sandbox and TestFlight testing](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/storekit/testing-at-all-stages-of-development-with-xcode-and-the-sandbox)

## Proposed bounded route, not executed

1. After Apple's current compliance and availability work, read back build 1.0 (2) readiness, active agreements, products, a permitted region excluding France and mainland China, and whether a suitable TestFlight group and Apple-silicon testing are enabled. If needed, configure a narrowly scoped tester group and an owned tester only under the coordinated qualification task; do not assume one already exists. Avoid re-uploading the existing build.
2. Use a distinct owned non-complimentary Vipi test identity and isolated app/device state. Neither the held company sender nor the held Beta recipient is suitable: another device registration can replace a messaging device or wake the original outbox. The other existing reviewer identities have complimentary Base and Bot access, which masks purchase grants. No suitable separately admitted non-reviewer identity has been established by this read-only audit. Normal verified signup and a narrowly scoped subject-admission update, followed by a separately scoped sandbox-user UUID update, would be necessary if none exists; preserve existing reviewers, tester and queued messages. No production allowlist or registration change is implied.
3. Check the actual product UI and server snapshot on the isolated test identity. The app requires a verified product, backend readiness and an inactive plan before purchase. During the account's 30-day cardless period Base is active, so current `Subscriptions.swift` `canPurchase` suppresses Base checkout. Bot mini is separately purchasable only with active Base and a ready Bot service; it can exercise its own eligible sandbox trial and receipt path, but does not establish Base purchase. A genuine Base purchase requires a verified inactive Base state under an appropriately isolated test setup or the natural trial end; do not change a real account's dates or bypass production gates merely to create a test.
4. Only after explicit coordination, use one Sandbox purchase on the isolated identity, observe the App Store-signed transaction and account-bound backend entitlement, then exercise restore with an appropriately designed clean same-account device context. A new device may replace a messaging registration, so define the test data and device handoff beforehand. Record renewal/cancellation/expiry separately; no repeated purchase or reset on an uncertain result. Leave the original queued message and its accounts untouched.

This document is a proposed route, not proof of group availability, an account, a purchase, a restore, App Review readiness, or an Apple production notification. No account, app, backend, App Store Connect, TestFlight, or purchase state was changed during this review.
