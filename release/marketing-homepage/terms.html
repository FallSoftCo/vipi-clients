Who provides Vipi

Fallible Software, Co. provides Vipi. By using Vipi, you agree to these product terms and Fallible Software’s Terms of Service. Those company terms cover the operation of our services, intellectual property and other general conditions. The license supplied with any separately licensed software or language data governs that material.

Vipi is for people aged 13 or older. Use your own account, keep your sign-in secure and protect access to your device. Your exact username can be used to find you. Share your invitation link with people you intend to hear from.

Chat, practice and pricing

The launch version offers free one-to-one messaging with friends, without payment details. Answering an optional funding question, choosing Skip or leaving a store rating does not start a purchase or subscription.

Write to your friends in your usual language. Shallow and Deep offer ways to practice on your own device. Translations, pronunciation and reading guides can be wrong. Hold a translated message to see the original. Check important wording with the sender before relying on it.

Some features depend on your app version, service availability, compatible device, downloaded language data or local voices. Sending and receiving messages requires a connection. A message can expire while someone is offline.

What you can share

Share only content you have the right to send. Respect people’s privacy and their choice to stop hearing from you. These rules apply to direct messages, group conversations and any media, profile or invitation you share.

Do not send threats, harassment, hateful or exploitative material, scams, spam, sexual content involving minors, nonconsensual intimate material, or other unlawful content. Do not impersonate someone, distribute malware, interfere with the service or evade another person’s block or an account restriction.

You can block another user and report selected content in the app. Review the material before submitting it. Authorized reviewers may act on a report, including restricting accounts. We consider the evidence before taking action. For a safety concern or to ask us to review a restriction, email one@fallsoft.co. Vipi is not an emergency service.

Devices, backups and closing your account

Vipi supports one active messaging device per account. Replacing a phone changes its encryption identity. Compare safety numbers with your friend through a channel you trust if you need to verify the change.

Each phone holds its own history. Vipi does not keep a cloud copy of your complete conversation. To keep completed direct-message history, export an encrypted backup and save its password somewhere safe. Vipi cannot reset that password or recover an unreadable backup.

You can close Vipi in Settings or delete your Vipi account online. A friend’s copy and files you exported stay with their owners. If you previously subscribed through a store, manage any renewal in Apple subscriptions or Google Play subscriptions. Deleting Vipi does not cancel an earlier store subscription.

Questions and privacy

For help using Vipi, visit Vipi Help or write to one@fallsoft.co. Do not send passwords, backup files or private conversation history by email.

The Vipi Privacy Notice explains delivery records, on-device translation, reports and deletion. Contact privacy@fallsoft.co for privacy requests.
