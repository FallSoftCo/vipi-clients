Website privacy

When you visit mambovipi.app

We log IP addresses, requested URLs, browser details and referrers for security. Logs rotate daily, and we keep 14 archived rotations. We don’t use advertising trackers or analytics cookies.

When you ask for the launch email

If you submit the email form, we store your email address, the time you confirmed consent, and the page and campaign details that brought you to the site, such as an Instagram post identifier in a link. We use this only to send Vipi launch and early-access updates and to understand which launch content is useful. We do not sell it or share it for advertising.

We keep launch email entries until you ask us to remove you or until 12 months after launch, whichever comes first. Ask us to remove your entry or unsubscribe at privacy@fallsoft.co. We also record page views and launch email interactions with campaign details in a first-party database. These records use a temporary in-browser identifier rather than a cookie, contain no message content, and are deleted after 180 days.

When you use the Vipi app

When you message a friend, the Signal Protocol protects your conversation with end-to-end encryption. Vipi can see who sent a message, who it’s for, when it was sent and whether it arrived. Push alerts from Vipi contain no message text.

Your phone translates your messages and reads them aloud on your device. It may need to download a language or a voice first. Language and notification providers may receive device details, language selections and error reports.
