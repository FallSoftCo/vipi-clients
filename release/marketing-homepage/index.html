Type in your language

Type the message the way you normally would. Vipi keeps the original and prepares the practice version on your phone.

Shallow immersion

The message stays in your language, with one new word in the language you’re learning. Words you’ve already seen can appear again.

Deep immersion

All messages in the conversation appear in the language you’re learning. Press and hold a message to see the original.

Hear any message

Tap Listen to hear a message in the language you’re learning.

Build a streak

A day counts when you and your friend each send a delivered message. The count appears beside their name.

Chat in groups

Start a group from the Chats screen and invite your friends. Each message shows who sent it, and a streak day counts when two different members send a message.

Share images, video and voice notes

Send a photo or video with the camera button. Hold the microphone to record a voice note. Photos and videos open full-screen; voice notes play from the message.

All chats are encrypted by Signal protocol

Vipi uses the Signal Protocol. Messages and media are encrypted on your phone before they’re sent. Vipi’s relay cannot read them.

Use it in real life

Next time you see them, use what you’ve practiced — no app in between.
