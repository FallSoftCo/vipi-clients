What inspired Vipi

Studies of second-language learning find that people can pick up vocabulary while reading or listening for meaning. Vipi brings that kind of exposure to conversations you already have.

Vipi also takes a cue from Doug Engelbart’s idea of augmenting human intellect. Your phone helps you read and hear the language you’re learning while you carry on the conversation.

  • Engelbart’s 1962 report

It stays between you.

Vipi encrypts your chats end to end. Translation and speech happen on your phone, so your messages don’t have to go to a translation service.
