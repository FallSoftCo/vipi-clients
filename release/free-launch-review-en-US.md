# Review access for free friend chat

## Apple App Review notes

Choose a language you know and a practice language. English and Spanish work for the example. Continue to sign in with the existing review account supplied in the secure sign-in fields. Chatting with friends requires no purchase.

Open Chats. Open a conversation if the device already has one, or choose New chat. Returning friends are listed there by name. For a first connection, use an exact Vipi username or choose Invite a friend. The optional phone contacts flow explains how Vipi handles your address book before the system asks for access. You can cancel any invitation draft. Local history belongs to the active device. You can also try immersion in the example during setup without contacting anyone.

Choose Shallow at the top of the chat to see a practice word. Switch to Deep to read the whole message in the practice language. Tap the message to hear it, or press and hold the chat to reveal the original. A language or voice download may be needed. Settings contains history backup and Vipi account deletion.

## Release owner

### Optional feedback

Vipi is free. After someone has sent 20 messages across at least seven days, Chats can ask whether they would pay US$1 a month. Yes, Not sure, No and Skip all return to Chats. A choice records feedback only. Nobody needs to answer to keep chatting. The app requests the platform’s own review prompt separately, after at least 40 sends across 14 days, regardless of any survey answer. The store decides whether to display its prompt.

The pricing question needs a successful availability check on the versioned survey endpoint. Older relays and offline devices continue to support the rest of the journey. Verify the account-linked feedback disclosure and public privacy notice against the deployed version before enabling the question. Verify store review integration using the platform's test route.

An operator can count distinct participating accounts by answer without reading account identifiers. In the campaign below, exclude Skip from the denominator when reporting the percentage of respondents who answered Yes, and report skips separately. The sample covers people who met the chat threshold and answered. A Yes does not establish an actual subscription or conversion rate.

```sql
SELECT choice, COUNT(*) AS accounts
FROM survey_decisions
WHERE campaign = 'private-chat-usd1-v1'
GROUP BY choice;
```
