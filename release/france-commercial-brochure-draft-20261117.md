# Vipi — draft commercial product description

Working attachment for review; not a filing or evidence of launch.

Vipi is a consumer messaging app by Fallible Software, Co., a Delaware corporation. Friends chat as usual while selected supported words and short phrases in the messages a person reads switch into the language that person is learning. The friend does not need to know the learning language. Coverage varies by language and context. Users can listen to supported text and use reading guides where available.

Vipi includes private conversations between friends and an optional, separately identified chatbot. Friend messages use end-to-end encryption. Users can create an encrypted chat-history backup. The chatbot is a distinct service and its messages are sent to Vipi and its AI provider to produce replies; friend conversations are not sent automatically.

The intended offer is a 30-day account-bound Base period without an upfront payment method, followed by a separately chosen annual subscription. The optional Bot mini subscription is separate. The offer and launch availability must be confirmed against the finally deployed service and storefront before filing or distribution.
