# Independent critique of the nine first-person predicates

**Recommendation: test all nine as a bounded candidate after repairing the finite-scope guards below; approve none for shipping from the attestations alone.** No proposed unit needs wholesale rejection merely because it is a verb. Missing complete Swahili complement grammar or object agreement is not by itself a material meaning failure under the accepted native-grammar immersion policy; assess person, tense and predicate sense separately. Ordinary liking, wanting, needing, knowing, thinking, enjoyment, residence and teaching are useful content. Withhold the specifically unsupported occurrences; do not infer nine new concepts from nine spellings (`like` and `love` share penda).

## Concrete scope corrections before a candidate run

1. **Expand the external finite-operator check to cover copular inversion and remaining modal auxiliaries.** The proposal's left list omits `am/is/are/was/were` and modal `need/dare`; “Am I like my dad?” currently appears to be an unguarded preference match. Reject an immediately preceding contiguous operator using a declared finite set (existing list plus copular forms and need/dare, including contracted negative forms where tokenizer preserves them). Do not scan unrelated earlier sentences. This is a source construction guard, not a universal POS gate.
2. **For this positive-only increment reject immediately following contiguous `not`.** It catches “I need not go” and makes the no-negative promise concrete. This deliberately also withholds some interpretable forms such as “I think not”; record the omission rather than synthesizing negation. Ordinary “I don't need” and “I would like” do not contain the contiguous two-token key and must remain unmatched.
3. **Compute right context only across actual whitespace.** The old Python evaluator uses `right=keys[j]` without checking its intervening punctuation. Do not copy that behavior: “I live. In Paris?” must not borrow `in` from another sentence. Preserve exact source span and native whole-word geometry.
4. **Keep existing identifier/literal and enclosing-source masks over the entire recognized predicate/context**, not just the displayed part. `I like_music.txt`, code and mentions cannot authorize a unit. Preserve original case behavior and the explicit uppercase predicate rejection. Do not introduce a new blanket quotation veto; reversible quoted-label policy remains a separate issue.

No new target strings, grammar transforms, object agreement, embeddings or NLP are needed. A guard failure still records recognition and no approved output. Existing higher-priority reference/frame protection must remain authoritative; a new predicate must not override it.
