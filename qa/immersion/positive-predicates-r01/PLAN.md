# Whole first-person predicates: candidate qualification

Baseline is `dfacf0d`: Swahili starter 005, including the qualified hair-sense
hold, with the existing reference-first source-frame route. The private candidate
retains all 213 entries and adds nine finite first-person forms across eight
predicate families. Five families are new relative to this starter; like/love
share one family. Exact conjugations are retained primary dictionary evidence.
The finite English context permissions are authored and reviewed for this
diagnostic, not asserted as previously attested complete bilingual clauses.

The candidate includes I like/love/want/need/know/think/enjoy/live/teach.
Preference needs `to`, a declared nominal/activity head, or a declared determiner
plus nominal head. Enjoyment needs one of seven ordinary activity heads. Living
needs one of four following prepositions. Unknown complements are withheld.
External finite operators, contiguous negative `not`, unwanted `for`, ALLCAPS
predicate tokens, broken word boundaries, punctuation-crossing context and
protected context cannot authorize these units. Swahili complements and object
agreement are outside this mixed-language increment.

A complete predicate costs two native source words. Recognition and its mask
survive guard, known-concept and budget withholding; an inner pronoun or isolated
verb cannot be rescued to fill the budget. This deliberately can reduce flips in
short messages at low density. Existing exact references and longer frames keep
priority. Unrelated words share the existing total budget. Following context is
retained in the trace/recognition; only the two-word focus contributes to depth.

Both native runs use the same exposed 80 conversation messages, English→Swahili
at .25 and .75: 160 default-route outputs per platform, with model availability
forced unavailable and no target/span/provider injection. The previous qualified
capture supplies the baseline. Thirty-seven separate authored controls inspect
ordinary meaning, unsupported senses, negative/inverted constructions, literals,
repetition, low budget, caller exclusion and an explicit source mask. Their raw
starter traces are separate from actual default-route provenance. Some outputs
are explicitly marked for semantic review, not assumed to be correct because a
mechanical count passes.
