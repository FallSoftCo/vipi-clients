## Exact native admission and precedence proposal

Keep exact full-reference priority. At the new test-only provider seam, find full literal/slot expansions inside the original sentence using native complete-word endpoints; keep original UTF16 context and focus ranges. Matching is case-exact and does not normalize punctuation, insert spaces or cross sentence/newline barriers. The existing complete literal protections and caller source masks apply to the entire matched context and emitted focus. Thus doctor cannot match doctorate; station inside station.txt and coffee inside coffee_today.txt cannot authorize output. Contractions and partial native words are not guessed.

Resolve overlapping focuses by longest matched context first, then longest complete focus. Equal-strength competing meanings/faces/readings abstain; preserve all competitors in trace. A stronger recognized frame keeps its focus mask even if its permission, known-state, target identity or budget prevents output. In particular `need a phone charger` cannot fall back to the shorter `phone charger` context to emit only charger when the full two-word focus cannot fit. Nonoverlapping contexts remain independent; exact repeated occurrences have separate geometry/provenance. No inference of target-side ownership from source matching.

Use one native budget for frames plus ordinary starter candidates, cap 128 admitted units, no overlaps or partial units. Frame candidates precede word fallback; the existing starter rank/category ordering remains unchanged inside the fallback tier. Preserve protections even at zero density. Existing unrelated source-name/literal guards are not lifted by a longer match. A reviewed source-frame permission must explicitly identify the permitted complete focus; it does not globally approve that spelling.
