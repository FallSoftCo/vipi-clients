# Reviewed source contexts enabled in both native clients

The normal English→Japanese and English→Swahili providers now use the qualified
source-context packs. Existing exact and composed sentence references retain
priority. On a reference miss, longer reviewed phrase contexts and ordinary
starter vocabulary share one native-word budget. Withheld contexts keep their
source masks; a shorter match cannot bypass them. Original messages and Copy
original remain unchanged.

Each pack contains 41 reviewed patterns and 390 finite variants. Together with
their manifests they add 106,428 bytes. These are bounded contexts, not 780 new
meanings. The deterministic compiler preserves seed record provenance and an
explicit approval registry. Only two previously reviewed Swahili faces change
case: `Sherehe` → `sherehe` and `Nambari ya simu` → `nambari ya simu`.

Both actual default native routes reproduce all 656 retained diagnostic rows,
allowing exactly eight displayed spans affected by those corrections. Root
independently compared complete outputs, readings, source geometry and route
origins across platforms. All translation-call counts are zero. No provider,
target sentence or spans were supplied to those default-route checks.

Both native conversation components demonstrate six automatic flips per pair
with fixed paragraph bounds, Japanese script/transliteration and Listen reaching
playback. Root individually inspected the three primary screenshots from each
platform. The views use a fixed synthetic message and the actual default
component; they do not qualify account navigation or acoustic correctness.

Apple additionally passed nine malformed-input conditions and two concurrent
messages sharing a provider. Android passed the original nine malformed-pack
checks and 14 literal tests. A final constructor-only Android amendment requires
an actual String reading field, matching Swift decoding: both frozen packs were
accepted and seven missing/non-string variants rejected. The selector and
content were unchanged; the preceding full comparison and UI were not replayed.
The final enabled source bytes match the qualified private copies. Android's
private integrity file has only an additional historical QA compatibility alias.

On the retained 80-message-per-pair diagnostic at density 0.75, the routing
increment increases messages with an immersion span from 54→59 for Japanese and
55→58 for Swahili, and covered native words from 72→102 and 79→110. These inputs
were already exposed to development and reviewers. This is neither an estimate
of broad conversation coverage nor a 90% accuracy or fluency claim. Prior
semantic disagreements and the quoted-prose policy remain in
`SEMANTIC_OUTCOMES.md` and the explicit approval registry. Durable familiarity,
additional language directions and broader semantic coverage remain open.

`root-acceptance.json` binds the activated files to the native results and
preserved holds. `APPLE_PRODUCTION_RESULT.md` and `ANDROID_PRODUCTION_RESULT.md`
retain platform details. Earlier failed/obstructed attempts and frozen scripts
are historical evidence; they must not be blindly replayed against changed state.

This is source activation, not customer deployment. No signing retry, iPad use,
provider mutation, store submission or public launch occurred. The Mac
signing/keychain hold and all other release gates remain unchanged.
