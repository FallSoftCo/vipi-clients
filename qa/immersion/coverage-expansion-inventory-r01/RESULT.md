### What is limiting now

**Focus/cohort selection is the first removable data restriction for Spanish.** Existing annotation contains 24,177 potential English content occurrences, while the previous training dataset deliberately retained only a newly admitted common-noun subset. Reusing those full annotations avoids another model pass and avoids permanently restricting expansion to source-name exceptions. The actual eligible linked yield across ordinary nouns, verbs, adjectives and adverbs is still unmeasured; this inventory does not convert POS counts into an expected gain.

**Missing target/form/link evidence remains a real second restriction.** The earlier 9,535-message loss audit found 3,190 messages without an eligible link, of which 2,971 already contained an in-scope noun/adjective/adverb. This cannot be dismissed as verb-only utterances. Its no-shared-head branch separated 316 contexts without supported source forms, 1,279 without supported target forms and 2,398 with forms but no shared link. Corpus witness/form mismatch, true missing relations, unsafe forms and explicitness must remain separate. The earlier source-name exception increased raw nonempty output from 4,557 to 5,855 messages but introduced season/title errors; it remains a rejected research arm, not the production baseline or evidence that bypassing a name guard is safe.

**Corpus sampling becomes the next issue only after measuring that expanded yield.** The other 32,885 Tatoeba pairs lack this annotation capture. Their existing alignments can be reused later; acquiring a larger training sample or annotating every retained endpoint is not necessary for the first expanded build. PGV news and Tatoeba sentence domains must remain separate, with no token-frequency or performance pooling to hide differences.

For EN→SW, the live source language is English: existing English native-token/Apple and OpenNLP qualification is relevant, but this research evidence has not become a deployed source-role path. EN→SW can expand evidence independently; SW→EN needs a separately qualified role/form route and its own source-message denominator. Neither inherits Spanish success.
