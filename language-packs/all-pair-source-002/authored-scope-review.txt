R02 DATA-ONLY REVIEW — frozen after root finite review, 2026-11-10

The candidate adds useful exact compounds outside the original need/search
templates, while addressing measured and reviewed r01 context failures. It
does not activate language data or change native code. Both platform agents
received exact hashes for separate native qualification. Production acceptance
remains pending actual mixed outputs and controls.

Scope:
1. Three greeting/thanks/farewell groups now declare whole_sentence, preserving
   their terminal punctuation and standalone-utterance meaning. Merely dropping
   punctuation would have admitted arbitrary embedded formulas; this revision
   instead requires exact native sentence content, with surrounding whitespace.
2. Original Chinese phrase variants ending at the focus have clause_or_end
   guards. This rejects continued compound words while permitting ordinary short
   clauses and clause-final punctuation. Bread retains guarded 吃面包 plus the
   explicit 吃面包的时候 variant. Tea/water use guarded 喝一杯茶 / 喝一杯水 plus
   their explicit when variants; no native word is split to manufacture a match.
3. Eight standalone compound groups: mailing address, phone charger, phone
   number, door key, hotel room, train station, train ticket, bus stop. Exact
   full compounds supply their own meaning cue; generic room/key/charger/etc.
   are not included. These use the same concept IDs and target readings as the
   corresponding reviewed grid items. The clause-final guard is intentionally
   not imposed on these self-disambiguating compounds with normal open grammar.
4. Japanese bare 駅 is withheld as a standalone source: 道の駅 is a common
   roadside-service-area counterexample that native token edges do not solve.
   The existing candidate source context 駅は was also narrowed to 電車の駅.
   Japanese target 駅 remains when another source language establishes railway
   meaning. Shipped en-ja/sw behavior is untouched.

Root inspected all 87 standalone source-language examples and target faces and
found no material meaning error for this next candidate. That finite review is
not production acceptance, native-human gold or a population-accuracy claim.
Examples are agent-authored, including naturally omitted Korean particles:

我把手机充电器落在楼上了。
Native word edges, protected literals, zero density and source language guards
remain mandatory. Attached-particle examples are explicitly negative controls.
