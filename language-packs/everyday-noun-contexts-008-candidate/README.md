# Private everyday noun-context proposal 008

**Inactive, unqualified candidate.** `build.py` writes only this directory. It
never changes a bundled manifest, pattern pack, index, native source or pin.

The proposal broadens three already accepted everyday senses: breakfast as a
meal, coffee as a drink, and water as a liquid. Explicit noun constructions such
as `for breakfast`, `a glass of water`, `une tasse de café`, `كوب ماء`, `吃早餐`
and `水をください` are reusable permission contexts, not complete message
translations. They were authored separately from the 330-message probe; the
English `some water` construction also occurs in that probe, so any post-change
probe replay is development regression evidence.

Every source form, target, meaning, reading, concept and original evidence
record is retained verbatim from its own accepted directional pattern.
`evidence.json` binds those exact files and patterns. New grammatical permission
is an author proposal, not independent linguistic validation. No target is
pivoted through English, reversed, regenerated, or guessed. No generic casing,
stemming, carrier, article removal, or morphology policy is introduced.

The builder omits constructions already covered by an identical-face local
permission. It produces 176 proposed patterns / 368 explicit contexts across
98 directions. German needs no additional permission for these concepts because
its accepted noun patterns already cover the proposed contexts. English→Japanese
and English→Swahili retain separate raw starters: the corresponding exact
non-bare frame records are absent, so this candidate does not invent them.
Those omissions are deliberate and do not remove advertised language pairs.

Ambiguous source words remain guarded: English breakfast can be a verb,
Spanish desayuno can mean “I have breakfast,” and café can denote an
establishment. The supplied constructions specify the meal/drink sense. Existing
Italian cibo/musica restrictions remain untouched. Native boundaries, density,
literal masks, detection, and normalized identity still apply. An Arabic article
or Korean particle attached to a noun is not made eligible by this proposal;
existing finite carrier evidence needs separate native-path diagnosis.

Before promotion: review the proposed contexts, capture native geometry, qualify
actual automatic/direct paths and Latin readings in both clients, retain
homograph and protected-literal controls, and bind the eventual output/index
hashes. Test different naturally phrased messages as well as the original floor
probe. Current targets/readings being accepted does not automatically approve
new source-context permissions or acoustics.
